Applied projects

  • Modeling California Energy Prices Using Markov ChainsPDF
    A four-regime Markov model fit to ~35M rows of hourly CAISO price data, using expected dwell times to locate the nodes where high prices persist.
  • Effect of Citronellal Odor Puffs on Cockroach NeuronsPDF
    Spike trains modeled as a non-homogeneous Poisson process with a smooth baseline and per-trial random effects; the stimulus effect is real but neuron-specific.
